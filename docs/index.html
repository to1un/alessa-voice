alessa-voice

Press a key, talk, press it again: the text is pasted where your cursor is. Fully local, for KDE Plasma on Wayland.

F8
Message…

How it works

  1. F8: the voice box appears and its LEDs follow your voice. The system volume is turned down.
  2. F8 again: the volume comes back and two lights meet in the middle while whisper.cpp transcribes on your GPU.
  3. The text is pasted at the cursor. Ctrl+F8 discards instead.

Requirements

KDE Plasma 6 (Wayland), a GPU with Vulkan, whisper-cpp, python-pyqt6, layer-shell-qt, pipewire, wl-clipboard, ydotool.

Install

git clone https://github.com/to1un/alessa-voice ~/projects/alessa-voice
ln -s ~/projects/alessa-voice/alessa_voice.py ~/.local/bin/alessa-voice
alessa-voice &

Then bind alessa-voice toggle and alessa-voice cancel to global shortcuts. Full steps in the README.
